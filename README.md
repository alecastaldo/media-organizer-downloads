# Media Organizer for Mac

Find **exact duplicate** photos and videos safely, fix **wrong dates** (for example on photos downloaded from iCloud), and **organize** your archive by date, trip and camera: iPhone, iCloud, DJI, GoPro, Insta360 and more.

Everything runs **only on your Mac**. Nothing is uploaded, and nothing is deleted without a way back.

## ⬇️ [Download Media Organizer](https://github.com/alecastaldo/media-organizer-downloads/raw/main/Media-Organizer.zip)

For Macs with macOS 11 or newer, Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) or Intel. Free while in testing.

## Install (5 minutes, only once)

1. Click **Download Media Organizer** above.
2. Open your **Downloads** folder. If you see `Media-Organizer.zip`, double-click it. You get a folder called **Media Organizer**.
3. Move the **Media Organizer** folder to your **Documents** or **Applications** folder.
4. Open it and **double-click `Media Organizer.command`**.

**The first time, macOS shows a warning**, because the app is not yet registered with Apple:

- **macOS 15 Sequoia or newer:**
  1. The message says Apple "could not verify" the file. Click **Done**.
  2. Open  → **System Settings → Privacy & Security**.
  3. Scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to "Media Organizer.command", then confirm with your password.
  4. Double-click `Media Organizer.command` again and click **Open**.
- **macOS 14 or older:** right-click (or Control-click) `Media Organizer.command` → **Open** → **Open**.

**If macOS offers to install the "Command Line Tools", click Install.** They are free from Apple and let the app run. Wait until they finish, then press **Enter** in the Terminal window.

The app opens in your web browser. A **Terminal** window opens too: that is the app's engine. Keep it open while you use the app, and close it when you are done.

**Permissions:**
- When macOS asks *"Terminal would like to access files on a removable volume"*, click **Allow**.
- To read external disks or the Photos library: **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access**, turn on **Terminal**, then restart the app.

## First steps (please start with a copy)

While the app is in testing, **try it first on a copy of a folder**, not on your only archive.

1. **Find duplicates:** add a folder and click **Find duplicates**. Nothing changes until you confirm. Extra copies go to a quarantine folder, and **Restore all** puts them back.
2. **Fix dates:** click **Check dates** first, which shows what would change without changing anything. You can undo afterwards.
3. **Organize archive:** click **Analyze my folders** to see the suggested structure, and **Preview** before copying or tidying up.

## Updates

Click **Check for updates** (top right in the app). The app updates itself and restarts; your settings and history are kept.
